About Gabbani Tech

A 1937 obsession with the counter, written in code

Gabbani Tech is a young company from Lugano. We build software for the businesses that live by their counter, from shops and delis to restaurants, and we start where we know it best.

Our roots

Born from a Lugano name

The Gabbani name has been part of Lugano since 1937 with butchery, deli, wine and hospitality, served across the old town. Decades at the counter teach you exactly what a shop owner needs to see, and when.

Gabbani Tech takes that instinct and turns it into tools. The first is simple: let owners see every sale the moment it happens, instead of waiting for the books to close.
