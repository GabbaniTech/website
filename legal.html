Overview

We take the protection of your personal data seriously. This website is intentionally minimal and collects as little data as technically possible.

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. The hosting provider may process technical access data (such as IP address, browser type and time of access) in server log files for the purpose of operating and securing the service.

Cookies & local storage

We do not use tracking cookies. We store a single technical flag in your browser's session storage to remember your language choice. It is cleared when you close the tab.
