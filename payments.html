Settlement handled by Open CryptoPay

Gabbani Tech does not touch the crypto itself. Every payment is routed and settled by Open CryptoPay, a product of DFX AG , the Swiss provider behind the open payment standard used across Lugano.

  • One QR code covers MyLuga, Bitcoin and Tether
  • Settlement runs through DFX AG, a regulated Swiss company
  • The paid sale lands on your dashboard like any other
