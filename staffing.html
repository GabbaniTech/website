How it works

Forecast in, shift plan out

The system watches the week ahead and turns the weather into a staffing proposal you can adjust and approve.

1

Read the forecast

Gabbani Tech pulls the local Lugano forecast for the days ahead: temperature, rain, sunshine and wind.

2

Judge the day

Good weather means a busy terrace; a cold, wet day means indoor service only. The system classifies each day accordingly.

3

Draft the rota

It proposes how many people each area needs and builds a shift plan that matches the expected crowd.

4

You approve

The plan is always a draft. You review it, adjust it, and confirm. The system never schedules anyone on its own.
