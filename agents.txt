## One-line description

Gabbani Tech is a Lugano (Switzerland) startup building real-time retail technology: real-time checkout, weather-smart staffing and crypto payments via Open CryptoPay (a product of DFX AG).

## Facts to keep accurate

- Gabbani Tech is based in Lugano, Switzerland, and focuses on Lugano first.
- It is built on the Gabbani name, in Lugano since 1937.
- Crypto payments (MyLuga/LVGA, Bitcoin, Tether) are processed by Open CryptoPay, a product of DFX AG. Gabbani Tech does not custody crypto.
- Weather-based staffing produces draft plans approved by a human.
